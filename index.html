Variable construction

Occupancy proxy

occupancy_proxy_30 = 1 - (availability_30 / 30), clipped to [0, 1].

availability_30 is the number of days in the next 30-day window that are listed as available. One minus that fraction gives an estimated occupancy rate for the window.

Limitations: does not distinguish host-blocked nights from guest-booked nights; does not capture dynamic nightly pricing or discounts; availability may lag actual bookings depending on host calendar management.

Revenue proxy

revenue_proxy_30 = price_num × (30 - availability_30).

Estimated gross revenue over 30 days, assuming the listed price applies to all booked nights.

Limitations: actual revenue will differ from advertised price due to discounts, cleaning fees, and length-of-stay pricing rules.

Reputation index

Composite of two standardized signals:

  • review_scores_rating_z: z-score of the structured star rating
  • cum_mean_sentiment_z: z-score of the cumulative VADER compound score across all reviews

reputation_index = mean(review_scores_rating_z, cum_mean_sentiment_z)

Both components are standardized first so neither dominates due to scale differences.

Key modeling decisions

Unit of observation: listing × scrape_date (listing-time panel)

Cross-section for main regression: most recent scrape per listing, to avoid serial correlation from repeated observations in pooled OLS without listing fixed effects.

Sentiment aggregation level: listing × calendar month, matched to scrape month for the panel merges.

Scrape sampling: every 4th available scrape date (SCRAPE_STRIDE = 4). Justified by runtime and the fact that listings change slowly across adjacent scrapes.

Review deduplication: Inside Airbnb re-exports all reviews in each snapshot. We deduplicate by review.id before sentiment scoring.
